# KAAL VIBHAG - MAIN MEMBERS

Character repository for the original Indian-mythological sci-fi organization **Kaal Vibhag — Department of Time**.

## Global visual rules

- Full-body front-facing character concept art on a dark background with soft golden rim light.
- Department palette: deep black, gold borders, gold inlay motifs.
- Loyalists: clean gold markings.
- Rebels: one hidden red mark.
- Neutral characters: one small silver mark.
- No logos, watermarks, modern city elements, or recognizable existing franchise designs.
